## 📌 Project Overview
This project applies the standard 6-stage data analysis lifecycle (Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyse, Share, Act) to human resources data. By evaluating employee demographics, performance metrics, and satisfaction scores, the goal of this classification project is to predict the likelihood of an employee leaving the company and to uncover the primary drivers behind attrition.

**Core Tools & Technologies:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn), SQL, Tableau / Power BI, Jupyter Lab.

---

## 1. Ask (Defining the Business Problem)
The HR leadership team needs actionable intelligence to reduce employee turnover, as high attrition rates lead to increased hiring costs and loss of institutional knowledge.

**Key Business Questions:**
* What are the most significant factors driving employee attrition?
* Can we build a predictive model to identify specific employees at high risk of leaving?
* What targeted retention strategies can be implemented based on these insights?

## 2. Prepare (Data Ingestion & Ecosystem)
* **Dataset:** HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset.
* **Storage & Access:** Raw CSVs ingested into Pandas DataFrames for local processing.
* **Security & Privacy:** Ensured absolute data confidentiality. All PII (names, employee IDs, social security numbers) were redacted prior to analysis to maintain ethical HR standards.

## 3. Process (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* **Handling Missing Values:** Audited the dataset for nulls; imputed missing performance scores with the departmental median.
* **Categorical Encoding:** Converted text-based categorical variables (e.g., Department, Job Role, Business Travel) into machine-readable formats using One-Hot Encoding and Label Encoding.
* **Feature Scaling:** Applied standard scaling to continuous variables like `MonthlyIncome` and `TotalWorkingYears` to prepare the data for distance-based machine learning algorithms.

```python
# Example Snippet: Categorical Encoding for Machine Learning
import pandas as pd
from sklearn.preprocessing import LabelEncoder

# One-Hot Encoding for nominal variables
df = pd.get_dummies(df, columns=['Department', 'BusinessTravel'], drop_first=True)

# Label Encoding for ordinal variables (e.g., Yes/No binary conversion)
le = LabelEncoder()
df['OverTime'] = le.fit_transform(df['OverTime'])
```

## 4. Analyse (Exploratory Data Analysis & Modeling)
Transitioned from exploratory visualisations to robust classification modeling to identify attrition risk.

* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Discovered that employees with low 'JobInvolvement' and 'MonthlyIncome' below the 25th percentile had a disproportionately high attrition rate.
* **Predictive Modeling:** Built and evaluated classification models (e.g., Logistic Regression, Random Forest) using `scikit-learn` to predict the binary `Attrition` target variable. Evaluated models based on Precision, Recall, and the F1-Score to minimize false negatives (failing to identify an employee who will leave).

```python
# Example Snippet: Random Forest Classification & Evaluation
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestClassifier
from sklearn.metrics import classification_report

X = df.drop('Attrition', axis=1)
y = df['Attrition']

X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(X, y, test_size=0.3, random_state=42)

# Initialize and train the model
rf_model = RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, random_state=42)
rf_model.fit(X_train, y_train)

# Predictions and Evaluation
predictions = rf_model.predict(X_test)
print(classification_report(y_test, predictions))
```

## 5. Share (Data Visualisation & Dashboarding)
* **Feature Importance Visualisation:** Extracted and plotted feature importances from the Random Forest model using Matplotlib and Seaborn to visually highlight that variables like `OverTime`, `MonthlyIncome`, and `Age` are the top predictors of turnover.

## 6. Act (Business Recommendations)
Based on the data lifecycle, the following strategic actions are recommended to the HR department:
1. **Revise Overtime Policies:** Since mandatory overtime is a leading indicator of burnout and attrition, HR should enforce stricter limits on consecutive overtime hours and introduce mandatory rest periods.
2. **Targeted Compensation Reviews:** Employees identified as 'High Risk' by the classification model, specifically those in the lower quartile of monthly income for their role, should be prioritized for market-rate salary adjustments.
3. **Proactive Check-ins:** Deploy the predictive model to generate a monthly "Retention Watchlist." Managers should conduct proactive, stay-interview check-ins with flagged employees before they receive competing job offers.
