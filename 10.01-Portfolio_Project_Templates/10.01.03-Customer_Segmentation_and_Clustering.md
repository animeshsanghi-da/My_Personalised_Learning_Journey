## 1. Ask (Defining the Business Problem)
The marketing department is experiencing declining ROI on promotional campaigns due to a "one-size-fits-all" approach and needs to identify high-value customer segments for targeted engagement.

**Key Business Questions:**
* Who are our most valuable customers (VIPs) driving the highest revenue?
* Which customer segments are at the highest risk of churning?
* How can we group customers with similar purchasing behaviours to optimize marketing spend?

## 2. Prepare (Data Ingestion & Ecosystem)
* **Dataset:** Retail Transaction / Customer Purchase History Dataset.
* **Storage & Access:** Raw transactional logs queried via SQL and loaded into Pandas DataFrames for advanced processing.
* **Security & Privacy:** Ensured strict compliance by utilizing anonymized Customer IDs in place of personal identifiers (names, emails, addresses).

## 3. Process (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* **Handling Anomalies:** Filtered out negative quantities (returns/cancellations) and zero-value transactions that would skew the monetary analysis.
* **Date Standardization:** Standardized invoice dates and established a reference date (usually the day after the last transaction in the dataset) to calculate Recency accurately.
* **RFM Feature Engineering:** Aggregated line-item transactional data up to the unique Customer ID level to compute the three core metrics:
  * **Recency:** Days since the last purchase.
  * **Frequency:** Total number of distinct orders.
  * **Monetary:** Total revenue generated by the customer.

```python
# Example Snippet: Calculating RFM Metrics using Pandas
import pandas as pd
import datetime as dt

# Assume 'snapshot_date' is 1 day after the maximum date in the dataset
snapshot_date = df['InvoiceDate'].max() + dt.timedelta(days=1)

rfm = df.groupby('CustomerID').agg({
    'InvoiceDate': lambda x: (snapshot_date - x.max()).days, # Recency
    'InvoiceNo': 'nunique',                                  # Frequency
    'TotalSpend': 'sum'                                      # Monetary
}).reset_index()

rfm.columns = ['CustomerID', 'Recency', 'Frequency', 'Monetary']
```

## 4. Analyse (Exploratory Data Analysis & Modeling)
Transitioned from descriptive RFM metrics to mathematical clustering using unsupervised machine learning.

* **Data Normalization:** Applied log transformations and StandardScaler from `scikit-learn` to normalize the RFM distributions, ensuring that high Monetary values did not mathematically overpower Recency and Frequency dimensions.
* **K-Means Clustering:** Deployed the K-Means algorithm to partition the customer base. Utilized the Elbow Method and Silhouette Scores to determine the optimal number of clusters (e.g., k=4).

```python
# Example Snippet: K-Means Clustering for Segmentation
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans

# Scale the data
scaler = StandardScaler()
rfm_scaled = scaler.fit_transform(rfm[['Recency', 'Frequency', 'Monetary']])

# Initialize and fit K-Means
kmeans = KMeans(n_clusters=4, random_state=42, n_init=10)
kmeans.fit(rfm_scaled)

# Assign the cluster labels back to the original dataframe
rfm['Cluster'] = kmeans.labels_
```

## 6. Act (Business Recommendations)
Based on the segmentation analysis, the following strategic actions are recommended for the marketing team:
1. **Cluster 0 ("Champions" - Low Recency, High Frequency, High Monetary):** Reward these VIP customers with exclusive early access to new product lines and loyalty program perks. Do not waste discount codes on this segment, as their purchase intent is already high.
2. **Cluster 1 ("At Risk" - High Recency, High Frequency, High Monetary):** These are former top buyers who haven't purchased recently. Immediately deploy personalized "Win-Back" email campaigns featuring aggressive, time-sensitive discounts to re-engage them before they churn to competitors.
3. **Cluster 2 ("New Customers" - Low Recency, Low Frequency, Low Monetary):** Focus on onboarding. Trigger an automated email sequence introducing the brand story and offering a small discount on their second purchase to increase their Frequency score.
