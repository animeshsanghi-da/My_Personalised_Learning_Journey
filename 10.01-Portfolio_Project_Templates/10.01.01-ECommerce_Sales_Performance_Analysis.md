## 📌 Project Overview
This project applies the standard 6-stage data analysis lifecycle (Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyse, Share, Act) to the Brazilian Olist E-commerce dataset. By processing over 100,000 order records, the goal of this analysis is to identify core drivers of customer satisfaction, optimize delivery timelines, and predict future sales trends using statistical modeling. 

**Core Tools & Technologies:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn), SQL, Tableau / Power BI, Jupyter Lab.

---

## 1. Ask (Defining the Business Problem)
Business stakeholders require visibility into logistical bottlenecks and sales performance to optimize operations.

**Key Business Questions:**
* What are the primary factors contributing to delayed deliveries?
* How does delivery time impact customer review scores?
* Can we predict estimated delivery durations based on freight value and geolocation?

## 2. Prepare (Data Ingestion & Ecosystem)
* **Dataset:** Olist E-Commerce Public Dataset (100,000+ rows).
* **Storage & Access:** Raw CSVs ingested and stored locally, explored via Pandas DataFrames.
* **Security & Privacy:** Ensured no Personally Identifiable Information (PII) was exposed; customer and seller IDs were anonymized.

## 3. Process (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* **Handling Missing Values:** Imputed missing timestamps using median delivery times per route; dropped rows with missing core financial metrics.
* **Data Type Corrections:** Converted all date strings to robust `datetime` objects for time-series extraction.
* **Feature Engineering:** Created new calculated columns:
  * `delivery_duration` (Actual Delivery Date - Order Purchase Date)
  * `estimated_vs_actual` (Variance between promised and actual delivery)


## 6. Act (Business Recommendations)
Based on the data lifecycle, the following strategic actions are recommended:
1. **Regional Hub Expansion:** Data shows significant delivery variance in northern states. Establishing a micro-fulfillment center in these regions will reduce freight costs and delivery times.
2. **Dynamic Delivery Estimates:** Implement the Linear Regression model into the checkout pipeline to provide more accurate delivery estimates, reducing customer dissatisfaction caused by missed deadlines.
3. **Targeted Seller Interventions:** Sellers with a consistent historical delay variance > 3 days should be flagged for logistical support or penalized in the search ranking algorithm.
