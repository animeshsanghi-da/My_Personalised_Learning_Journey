1. **Time-Series & Exploratory Analysis**
    - **Chart Types:** Line, Scatter, Histogram, Boxplot, ECDF, Pairplot, Heatmap, Hexbin, Dual-Axes, and Insets.
    - **When to use:** To analyze continuous sequence metrics, evaluate time-series trends, visualize data distributions, and identify relationships or correlations between variables.

2. **Financial Bridges**
    - **Chart Types:** Waterfall Chart.
    - **When to use:** To visualize absolute and relative financial flows, showing how starting values (like cash) are affected by positive and negative changes to reach a net ending total.

3. **Conversion Pipelines**
    - **Chart Types:** Conversion Funnel Chart.
    - **When to use:** To track volume counts and conversion percentages as users move through sequential pipeline stages, such as progressing from ad impressions to closed deals.

4. **KPI Targets & Performance Bands**
    - **Chart Types:** Bullet Chart.
    - **When to use:** To compare actual KPI metrics against predefined targets while contextualizing performance within qualitative bands (e.g., poor, satisfactory, good).

5. **Project Timelines**
    - **Chart Types:** Gantt Chart or Timeline.
    - **When to use:** To map out project schedules by visualizing specific tasks, their start days, total durations, and corresponding department assignments.

6. **Cohort Retention Matrix**
    - **Chart Types:** Cohort Heatmap.
    - **When to use:** To analyze user retention and engagement matrices across specific time-based cohorts over subsequent months.

7. **Flow Distributions**
    - **Chart Types:** Sankey Diagram.
    - **When to use:** To illustrate the volume and distribution of flows mapping from distinct source origins (like organic search or paid ads) to specific target outcomes.

8. **Multi-Attribute Benchmarks**
    - **Chart Types:** Radar / Spider Chart.
    - **When to use:** To evaluate and simultaneously compare multiple categorical attributes or performance benchmarks (e.g., price, scalability, UI/UX) across different products.

9. **Two-Point Comparisons**
    - **Chart Types:** Dumbbell & Slope Charts.
    - **When to use:** To highlight growth, decline, or relative changes between two distinct data points (such as comparing 2024 to 2025 values) across various categories.

10. **Spatial Maps**
    - **Chart Types:** Choropleth Maps.
    - **When to use:** To display spatial data and regional variations, such as mapping sales volumes by state using geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude).

11. **Hierarchical Categories**
    - **Chart Types:** Treemap & Waffle Charts.
    - **When to use:** To break down hierarchical, parts-to-whole categorical data, such as visualizing budgets and headcounts distributed by broad departments and specific sub-teams.
